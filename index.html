      CLAIM-SPECIFIC CONFIDENCE SCALE

      The letter grade belongs to the specific claim shown in a record—not to an entire chemical or product category.

      MISSION

      Help students and families understand exposure science without oversimplifying it.

      The Endocrine Exposure Atlas is being built as a public educational project that connects everyday exposure questions with endocrine biology and the quality of the underlying evidence.

      The goal is not to label every chemical as dangerous. It is to make the chain from source → exposure → mechanism → evidence → uncertainty understandable.
