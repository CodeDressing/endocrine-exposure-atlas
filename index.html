EVIDENCE STANDARD

Every claim should show what kind of evidence supports it.

A laboratory mechanism, an animal finding, a human association, and a replicated causal result are not interchangeable. The Atlas keeps those categories visible.

A
Established / high confidenceConverging high-quality evidence or authoritative assessment for the specific claim.
B
Moderate evidenceMeaningful evidence with remaining uncertainty, limitations, or incomplete replication.
C
Emerging / limited evidenceEarly human, animal, or mechanistic evidence that does not justify a strong conclusion.
?
Uncertain / unresolvedInsufficient, inconsistent, compound-specific, or context-dependent evidence.

MISSION

Help students and families understand exposure science without oversimplifying it.

The Endocrine Exposure Atlas is being built as a public educational project that connects everyday exposure questions with endocrine biology and the quality of the underlying evidence.

The goal is not to label every chemical as dangerous. It is to make the chain from source → exposure → mechanism → evidence → uncertainty understandable.
